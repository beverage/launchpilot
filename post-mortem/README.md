# Post-mortem

LaunchPilot was built in one day at the Accel AI Innovate hackathon in Paris on 22 October 2026, and finished in the top 8 of the final, but not in the top 3. This folder keeps everything we wrote about it afterwards, plus the design and the deck from the day.

| File | What it is | For whom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [postmortem.md](postmortem.md) | How we built it: the idea, what we shipped, the day hour by hour, what went well and what broke | Everyone, no technical background needed |
| [piloting-claude-code.md](piloting-claude-code.md) | How Alex piloted Claude Code: the preparation, running parallel sessions, who decided what, where we tripped up, and a checklist to copy | Anyone who wants to work this way |
| [example-prompt.md](example-prompt.md) | One real call to Claude, word for word: the instructions, the event, the tool calls and the alert | The curious |
| [design-in-plain-words.md](design-in-plain-words.md) | The non-technical design, as written at midday | Everyone |
| [design-technical.md](design-technical.md) | The technical design: architecture, events, data model, agents, the forecasting maths, deployment | Engineers |
| [pitch-deck.md](pitch-deck.md) | The 10-slide deck prepared for the final, with speaker notes, and the submitted one-page slide as an appendix | Everyone |
| [../docs/SCRIPT.md](../docs/SCRIPT.md) | The three-minute pitch script and the answers prepared for judges' questions | Presenters |

The two design documents are snapshots from around 13:00. The headline numbers changed at 14:30, when deliveries were moved to French working days: the final ones are 118 against 92 stockouts dated within a week, a 91% pump alert, and 437 k€ at risk for the best option. The postmortem and the deck carry those.

These files were exported from the team's working documents on 23 October 2026. Diagrams and slides are images; the deck images are renders of the slide files.
